The supplier, the goods and the amount are all real. Only the payment details change, so ordinary checks pass and the transfer can’t be recalled.

How it works

Four steps between an invoice and a payment.

1

Read

Paste an invoice or message. Fields are extracted by rules, or by AI that is cross-checked against the source text.

2

Check

A rule engine compares the wallet, network, sender and signature with your supplier’s verified record.

3

Confirm

Anything suspicious needs a callback or a second approver, or a swap to the registered wallet.

4

Prove

After paying, the transaction is verified on-chain and recorded in a shareable Proof Link.

What’s inside

Every way a payment can go wrong, checked before you send.

Instruction firewall

Catches a swapped wallet, a lookalike sender domain, reused invoice numbers and pressure language.

Wrong-network guard

Flags a Solana address on a TRON payment, a Bitcoin address under Ethereum, or USDT on a network Cleva doesn’t accept.

Address-poisoning guard

Warns when a new address has the same start and end as one you’ve used, the trick attackers plant in your history.

Supplier signatures

Suppliers can sign each instruction. Change the wallet or amount after signing and the signature breaks.

On-chain verification

Confirms the genuine Tether contract, destination, amount and confirmations, and catches fake tokens.

Proof Link and audit trail

A shareable record of who checked and confirmed what, backed by a tamper-evident hash chain.

Where it goes

From a safety check to payment infrastructure.

Today IntentLock protects one payer at a time. The same checks can become a shared layer that wallets and fintechs build on.

Verified payee identity

Suppliers publish their payment details once. Every buyer checks against them, like sender verification for email.

Payment-security API

One call returns a verdict. Wallets, marketplaces and fintechs embed it before the send button.

Approval layer for AI agents

As software starts paying on our behalf, it needs the same intent check a careful human would run.
